About NEHAM

A rights-led approach to safer communities.

NEHAM is positioned as an India-wide organisation working across safety, rights, prevention, protection, education, support and opportunity.

Our purpose

Build the knowledge, systems and confidence people need to act.

Our work connects people to information, institutions to better practice, and communities to practical ways of preventing harm. We work with women, children, young people, parents, educators, employers, government, NGOs, donors and community leaders.

Our programmes are designed to be accessible, evidence-informed and grounded in the Indian context.

Principles

How we work.

Safety with dignity

People affected by violence should be treated with respect, privacy and care.

Child-centred protection

Children's best interests, participation and protection remain central to child-focused work.

Prevention + response

Awareness matters, but it must connect to clear procedures, support and accountability.

Inclusion

We design for different ages, abilities, backgrounds and lived experiences.

Partnership

Safety is a shared responsibility across families, institutions, communities and systems.

Transparency

We aim to publish clear information about programmes, resources, partnerships and finances.
