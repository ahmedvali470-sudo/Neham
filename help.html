Need help now?

Get immediate help or find a support pathway.

If you are in immediate danger, use the official emergency services below. NEHAM's website should complement—not replace—official emergency response.

Emergency

112 — Emergency Response

For urgent police, fire, medical and other emergency assistance in India.

Call 112
Women

181 — Women Helpline

Government women helpline for assistance, information and referral support.

Call 181
Children

1098 — Child Helpline

Government child helpline for children needing care, protection or assistance.

Call 1098
Workplace

Workplace sexual harassment

The Government's WCD site currently provides access to SHe-Box for workplace-related sexual harassment complaints.

Open SHe-Box
If you are helping someone else, protect their privacy, listen without blame, avoid promising outcomes you cannot guarantee, and use appropriate emergency or professional services where needed.
