Our impact model

Problem → intervention → outcome → learning.

Each major programme should explain the problem, what NEHAM did, who participated, what changed, and what the organisation learned.

  • Define the need and baseline.
  • Describe the intervention and partners.
  • Report measurable outputs and outcomes.
  • Include participant voice where consent permits.
  • Publish limitations and learning.
