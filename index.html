What we do

From awareness to action.

NEHAM brings together prevention, protection, education, support and community participation so that safety is not treated as a single-issue problem.

01

Protect children

Child protection, POCSO awareness, school safety, parent and educator capacity building, and child-friendly rights education.

Explore child protection →
02

Strengthen women's safety

Work on gender-based violence, domestic violence, public safety, workplace safety, legal awareness and survivor-centred support pathways.

Explore women's safety →
03

Build safer institutions

Training and advisory support for schools, colleges, corporates, NGOs, government stakeholders and community organisations.

Our approach

Knowledge that people can use.

We turn complex rights, laws and safety practices into accessible resources, workshops, stories, comics and community tools.

A

Awareness

Clear, age-appropriate information about rights, consent, safety and available support.

B

Capacity

Practical training for educators, employers, frontline workers, communities and young people.

C

Support

Signposting to emergency services, legal and psychosocial support and appropriate referral pathways.

D

Action

Partnerships, advocacy, research, campaigns and community-led solutions that can be sustained.
