Our work

Programmes built around real-world safety.

NEHAM's programme architecture covers prevention, protection, support, institutional strengthening and public education.

01

Child protection & POCSO

For children, parents, schools, colleges, educators, staff and district-level stakeholders.

POCSO awareness

Accessible training on child sexual abuse prevention, reporting responsibilities, child-friendly processes and stakeholder roles.

School safety

Whole-school safety approaches covering physical, socio-emotional and institutional safeguards.

Parent & educator training

Practical sessions on recognising concerns, responding appropriately and creating safer environments.

02

Women's safety & gender justice

Domestic violence awareness

Rights awareness, safety planning information, referral pathways and community education.

Workplace safety & POSH

POSH awareness, committee capacity building, employee education and safer workplace practices.

Public & digital safety

Campaigns and resources addressing harassment, bystander action, digital risks and safer public spaces.

Legal literacy

Plain-language information about relevant laws, processes, rights and support systems.

Survivor support pathways

Information and referral pathways for legal, psychosocial and institutional support.

Economic empowerment

Where relevant to programmes, connect safety and agency with skills, livelihoods and opportunity.

03

Training, research & institutional support

Schools & colleges

POCSO, child protection, gender, consent, bystander intervention and safety systems.

Corporates

POSH, inclusion, awareness, policy implementation and workplace safety training.

Government & NGOs

Capacity building, resource development, research and community-facing programmes.

Research & data

Evidence-led research, needs assessments, programme learning and public education.

04

Comics & creative education

Use stories and visual learning to make rights and safety easier to understand.

Rights comics

Short illustrated explainers for children, young people, parents and educators.

Campaign toolkits

Posters, social assets, workshop prompts and discussion guides.

Learning packs

Downloadable resources that organisations can use in classrooms and community sessions.
